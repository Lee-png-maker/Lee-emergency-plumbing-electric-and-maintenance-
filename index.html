FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions from customers.

Do you handle emergency plumbing problems?

Yes. You can contact us for urgent plumbing enquiries such as burst pipes, leaks, blocked toilets and geyser problems. Availability depends on location and scheduling.

Do you provide electrical services?

Yes. We handle electrical repair and maintenance enquiries such as power faults, tripping circuits, plugs, switches, lighting and fault finding.

Do you repair geysers?

Yes. Contact us with details of the geyser problem. The required repair or replacement can be assessed before work proceeds.

How much does an emergency call-out cost?

Pricing depends on the location, time, type of emergency, materials and work required. Contact us for a quotation.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Yes. We accept residential, office, shop and general property maintenance enquiries.

Can I send pictures on WhatsApp?

Yes. Photos can be useful for understanding the problem before visiting the property. Send them through WhatsApp after contacting us.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve customers across Gauteng, including Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion, Midrand and Tembisa, subject to availability.
