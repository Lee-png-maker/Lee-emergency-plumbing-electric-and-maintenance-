Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide emergency plumbing assistance?

Yes. Contact 068 868 5355 and explain the plumbing problem and your location.

Do you assist with leaking pipes?

Yes. Contact us regarding leaking pipes, taps, water connections, valves and related plumbing problems.

Do you assist with geysers?

Yes. Contact us regarding geyser problems, leaks, maintenance and installation enquiries.

Do you provide electrical services?

Electrical service enquiries include fault finding, electrical panels, wiring and maintenance.

Do you provide waterproofing?

Yes. Waterproofing enquiries include roof waterproofing, roof leak repairs, wall waterproofing, damp protection, bathroom and shower waterproofing, balcony waterproofing, crack sealing and joint sealing.

Yes. Contact Lee regarding roof leaks and roof waterproofing requirements. Send photographs of the affected area on WhatsApp where possible.

Do you waterproof bathrooms and showers?

Yes. Contact us regarding bathroom and shower waterproofing enquiries, especially where water is penetrating surrounding walls or floors.

Yes. Contact us regarding general property repairs, handyman work, building maintenance and property improvement requirements.
