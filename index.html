Frequently Asked Questions

Do you assist with emergency plumbing?

Yes. Contact 068 868 5355 and explain the plumbing problem. Photos can also be sent through WhatsApp.

Can I contact you about a leaking pipe or tap?

Yes. Leaking pipes, taps and other plumbing problems can be discussed by phone or WhatsApp.

Do you provide geyser services?

Yes. Contact Lee with details of the geyser problem so the required service can be discussed.

Do you assist with electrical problems?

Electrical fault-finding, inspections and electrical service enquiries can be made by contacting 068 868 5355.

Do you provide waterproofing?

Waterproofing enquiries include roof waterproofing, roof leaks, wall waterproofing, damp protection, bathrooms, showers, balconies, cracks and joints.

Can you help with roof leaks?

Yes. Contact us with information and photos of the roof leak where possible.

Do you assist with damp problems?

Yes. Wall waterproofing and damp-related property problems can be discussed with our team.

Do you waterproof bathrooms and showers?

Yes. Bathroom and shower waterproofing enquiries are welcome.

Do you provide property maintenance?

Yes. General property repairs and maintenance enquiries can be made through the website, phone or WhatsApp.

Which areas do you cover?

Services are available across areas of Gauteng including Johannesburg, Pretoria, Midrand, Centurion, Ekurhuleni, Kempton Park, Benoni, Boksburg, Edenvale, Germiston, Alberton, Sandton, Randburg, Roodepoort, Soweto and Tembisa. Contact us to confirm availability.

Can I send photos of the problem?

Yes. WhatsApp photos or videos can help explain the problem before arranging a service.
